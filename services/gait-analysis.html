What does your walk say about you?

At Lincoln Foot and Ankle Center, we can digitally isolate and track 8 key foot landmarks to understand how you walk. With data gathered using Metascan, Dr. Hoffmann and Dr. Lagdaan can scientifically and accurately prescribe the right custom foot orthotic to help you keep your feet healthy and live an active lifestyle.
