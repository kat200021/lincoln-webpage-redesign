Meet Our Physicians

Lincoln Foot & Ankle Center provides both surgical and non-surgical care of the foot and ankle in Chicago.

Founding Physician · DPM

Dr. Kaye Lagdaan

Dr. Kaye Lagdaan is living her dream as the founding physician of Lincoln Foot and Ankle Center. Her vision was to create a practice where patients feel cared for and listened to — expert care in a close-knit, personalized environment with no shortcuts when it comes to specialized medical care.

Focus: Non-surgical treatment of the foot and ankle

View Full Profile →
Foot & Ankle Specialist · DPM

Dr. Heidi Hoffmann

Dr. Hoffmann joined Lincoln Foot and Ankle Center in 2017. Since 2004, she has provided quality podiatric care in the Chicagoland area with a focus on wellness and a personal touch. She provides both surgical and non-surgical care and is board certified by the American Board of Multiple Specialties in Podiatry.

Focus: General podiatry, sports medicine & surgical care

Community

Proud to Be Active in Our Community

Getting our patients back to their full and active lifestyles without foot or ankle pain is our goal. We love to see our patients get back to doing the things they love: taking long walks in the neighborhood, playing in little leagues, running charity races and out and about in the shops and restaurants in Northcenter.

Year after year, we proudly participate in the Northcenter Chamber of Commerce, support Friends of Ravenswood, sponsor a Welles Park Little League team and sponsor the Ravenswood 5K.
