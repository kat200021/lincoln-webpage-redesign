About Dr. Lagdaan

Dr. Kaye Lagdaan is living her dream as the founding physician of Lincoln Foot and Ankle Center. Her vision was to create a practice where patients feel cared for and listened to… where she can provide expert care of the foot and ankle in a close-knit and personalized environment that makes sure no shortcuts are taken when it comes to specialized medical care. Dr. Lagdaan focuses on non-surgical treatment of the foot and ankle.

Hospital staff & memberships

Dr. Lagdaan is on staff at Saint Joseph Hospital a nationally ranked and award winning hospitals on Chicago’s north side. Dr. Lagdaan is a member of the American Podiatric Medical Association, the Illinois Podiatric Medical Association, and the Northcenter Chamber of Commerce.

Education & Training

Dr. Lagdaan was born and raised in Florida, graduated from the University of Miami with a degree in biology and business, and moved to Chicago to complete her graduate degree at Dr. William M. Scholl College of Podiatric Medicine at Rosalind Franklin University of Medicine and Science. She completed a prestigious and competitive 3-year residency in podiatric medicine and surgery at Advocate Illinois Masonic Medical Center.

Outside the Office

Outside the office, Dr. Lagdaan enjoys reading, traveling with her husband and two daughters and trying new restaurants.
