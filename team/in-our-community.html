Proud Neighbors in Northcenter

We love being in the Northcenter neighborhood and supporting the charities, schools and businesses in our community. It is one of the joys of being a doctor when we see our patients go on to play in little leagues, run races and get back to living their lives without foot or ankle problems holding them back.

2019 Ravenswood Run

Thanks to the 3,200 racers who supported the All Saints Food Pantry in Ravenswood. It was a beautiful spring race day! Lincoln Foot & Ankle Center is proud to sponsor a race that supports a great cause in our neighborhood.

2019 Hop ‘N’ Stop in Northcenter

Lincoln Foot & Ankle Center was a proud sponsor of the 2019 Hop ‘N’ Stop event in our neighborhood presented by the Northcenter Chamber of Commerce.

2019 Welles Park Youth Baseball

Good foot and ankle health is important at all ages. Lincoln Foot & Ankle Center is proud to support the Welles Park Youth Baseball Mariners.
