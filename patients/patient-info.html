Getting Ready

How to prepare for your first visit

We take time to learn your medical history and introduce you to Dr. Lagdaan or Dr. Hoffmann. Print your forms, bring your documents, and you’ll walk in ready for a thorough first exam.

  1. 01

    Patient forms

    Print and complete this form before your appointment so we can spend more time on your care — and less on paperwork.

    Patient Information Form Bring a completed copy to your first visit
  2. 02

    What to bring

    Being prepared helps your doctor have everything needed for an accurate diagnosis — and helps you feel more at ease.

    • Photo ID, such as a driver’s license
    • Insurance card
    • Referrals, if required
    • Copay
  3. 03

    What to expect

    You can expect to share your medical history and symptoms, then have an x-ray, a biomechanical exam, and a review of your shoegear.

    • List of medications, allergies, and surgeries
    • Pharmacy information, if a prescription is needed
    • Time to meet your doctor and our team
